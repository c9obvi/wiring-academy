The enable Y-cable · the 10k resistor, start to finish

One small cable. You build it on the table, check it with the meter, then plug in its four ends. That's the whole job. (This page replaces sections 3 and 4 of the driver page.)

1 · The circuit (what it does)

In plain words: R_EN and L_EN are the driver's "allowed to drive" inputs. D4 is the ESP32 saying yes (3.3V) or no (0V). But while the ESP32 is starting up, unplugged or off, D4 says nothing: the wire "floats", and the driver can read random noise as yes. The 10k resistor is a weak spring pulling the line to "no" (ground). When the ESP32 says yes, it easily overpowers that weak spring. As a firmware person: it's an external pull-down, so EN reads LOW whenever the GPIO isn't actively driven.

2 · The finished cable, lying on your table

One joint (the "knot") where four things meet: three wires and one leg of the resistor. The resistor's other leg goes to the ground Wago: straight in if the Wago is right next to the knot (a resistor leg is solid wire, so no ferrule), or through a short extra wire with a ferrule if it's further away. The picture shows the long way.

3 · Build it (off the car, nothing powered)

3 female-female jumper wiresone 10k resistor (stripes: brown, black, orange, gold)one extra piece of jumper wire (~10 cm)heat shrink: 1 fat piece (~2 cm), 1 thin piecesoldering iron + solder1 white ferrule + crimpertape + pen
Prove it with the meter before it goes anywhere near the car:
• Beep mode: touch any two of the three plug ends (D4, R, L) together with the probes: beeps. All three are joined.
• Ω mode (20k range if it asks): one probe in any plug end, the other on the ferrule (or the bare resistor leg): about 10 kΩ (9.5 to 10.5). That's the resistor, sitting between the knot and ground.
If both are right, the cable is right.
Reads 0 or beeps from a plug end to the ferrule? The resistor got bypassed: a strand from the knot touches the ferrule wire. Re-do that heat shrink. Reads nothing (OL) from a plug end to the ferrule? The resistor's leg isn't soldered to the extra wire. Re-solder it.

4 · Where its four ends go

Everything OFF first (battery Wago open, so the ESP32 is dark too). Never plug or unplug on live pins.
Final check, still OFF: meter on Ω, one probe on the ESP32's D4 pin, the other on the ESP32's GND pin (left column, 2nd): about 10 kΩ. Not 0, no steady beep.
Then turn it ON, check that the dashboard is back, and message Claude "go". I'll drive it from here with no motors connected, and you measure R_EN: about 3.3V while driving, 0V when stopped.
