Driver ↔ ESP32 · the small signal wires

Classic ESP32 (DOIT 30-pin) held USB up · rear BTS7960 driver · logic ground on a 5-port Wago. Do this with everything OFF (battery Wago open).

1 · Every connection, one row each

Tick each row as it's done. The number in the circle matches the pin maps in section 2.

Leave empty: the driver's R_IS and L_IS pins. Nothing goes on them.

2 · Where the pins physically are

Go by the labels printed on your boards; the driver's header has a label next to each pin. Count ESP32 pins from the USB end.

3 · Check, then power on

Still OFF for these three checks (meter on beep / Ω).
